When you finish this page, you have opened the channel list and know what
a new org looks like before any channel exists.

**Who:** `owner` or `admin`.

## Prerequisites

A verified account and an organization. Free plans allow two channels.

## Steps

1. In the sidebar, open **Channels**. An empty org opens **Create Channel**
   / **Choose a Channel Type**.

![Docs Demo Create Channel picker. Business channels: WhatsApp Business, Telegram, Web Chat Widget, Pages Messenger, Instagram Business. Enterprise: HubSpot, Slack, Discord, Microsoft Teams.](https://docs-cdn.cerebronix.com/guides/getting-started/02-channels-create.png)

2. Pick a type from that grid. The labels on the page are the source of
   truth.
3. Complete the provider connect screen. If Meta or another OAuth host
   blocks the flow, stop. Do not fake a connected state.

Expected result: the dashboard **Usage & plan limits** tile increments
**Channels**.

WhatsApp template send and campaigns need WhatsApp opt-in. Open
**WhatsApp Templates** in the sidebar after a WhatsApp channel exists,
and use the Groups tab for membership.
