Cerebronix is the operator console for knowledge, AI agents, and customer
channels. You connect sources, bind an agent, and talk to customers on
WhatsApp, the web widget, and other channels from one organization.

This site is the product guide. The public API contract lives on the
developer platform.

When you finish this page, you know which host to open and what you are
operating.

## What you operate

| Area | Where |
|---|---|
| Organization, credits, inbox, agents, channels | `https://cerebronix-dev.cerebronix.com` locally, or production `https://cerebronix.com` |
| Public API, keys, webhooks, MCP | [Developer platform](https://developer.cerebronix.com/docs/current/overview) |

Cookies for the local console use `COOKIE_DOMAIN=cerebronix-dev.cerebronix.com`.
Do not rely on `localhost:3000` for a signed-in session.
