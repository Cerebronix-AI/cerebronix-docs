Organization owners and administrators can invite team members and assign role-based permissions across platform resources.

## Team Member Roles

Members can be assigned one of four standard organization roles:

| Role | Permissions |
|---|---|
| `owner` | Complete administrative, member management, and billing control. |
| `admin` | Manage agents, data sources, communication channels, and team settings. |
| `developer` | Configure AI agents, manage knowledge sources, and provision API keys. |
| `viewer` | Read-only access to conversation histories, contacts, and analytics. |

## Managing Team Access

Team invitations and role assignments are managed under **Organization Settings > Team Members**. When invited, team members receive an email link to verify their account and join your organization workspace.
