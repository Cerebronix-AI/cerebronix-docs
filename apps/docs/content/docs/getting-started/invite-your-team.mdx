As your automated support and conversational commerce operations grow, you will want colleagues across customer support, marketing, engineering, and finance to collaborate in your Cerebronix workspace.

Cerebronix provides a robust **Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)** model and seat management system, ensuring team members have the exact permissions necessary for their duties while safeguarding billing and security settings.

This guide walks through inviting team members to **Apex Retail Global** and configuring their access levels.

---

## The Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) Matrix

Cerebronix defines four primary team roles:

| Capability / Resource | Owner | Admin | Developer | Viewer (Operator) |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| **Workspace Ownership & Deletion** | Yes | No | No | No |
| **Plan Subscriptions, Cards & Invoices** | Yes | View Only | No | No |
| **Invite Members & Modify Roles** | Yes | Yes | No | No |
| **Create & Configure AI Agents** | Yes | Yes | Yes | Read Only |
| **Add & Re-index Knowledge Sources** | Yes | Yes | Yes | Read Only |
| **Connect & Configure Channels** | Yes | Yes | Yes | Read Only |
| **Generate Developer Public API Keys** | Yes | Yes | Yes | No |
| **Omnichannel Operator Inbox (`/chat`)** | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| **Human Operator Takeover** | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| **Customer Contacts & Groups** | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| **Native Store & Product Catalog Management** | Yes | Yes | Yes | View Orders |

<Callout type="info">
  **Security Principle:** Human support representatives and contact center operators should be assigned the **Viewer** role. This grants full access to the live omnichannel inbox (`/chat`) and customer conversation management without granting permission to alter agent prompts or modify billing.
</Callout>

## Prerequisites

To invite colleagues or modify permissions:
1. You must be logged into an account holding either the **Owner** or **Admin** role in the target workspace.
2. Your organization must have available team seats according to your current plan tier.

---

## Step-by-Step Walkthrough

<Steps>

### Open the Team Members Console

1. In the [Cerebronix Console](https://cerebronix.com), ensure your active organization is **Apex Retail Global**.
2. In the left navigation menu, click **Team Members** (`/team`).
3. The page displays:
   - **Active Members List:** Current team members, their email addresses, and assigned roles.
   - **Seat Usage Indicator:** The number of seats currently occupied versus your plan limit (e.g., `2 of 5 seats used` or `Unlimited seats`).

### Send an Invitation

1. Click the **Invite Member** button in the top-right corner.
2. In the invitation modal:
   - **Email:** Enter the corporate email address of your colleague (e.g., `sarah.ops@apexretail.example.com`).
   - **Role:** Select the appropriate role from the dropdown:
     - `owner` — Co-ownership, billing, and organizational transfer.
     - `admin` — Team administration and operational settings.
     - `developer` — Technical configuration, knowledge ingestion, and API keys.
     - `viewer` — Support operator for live chat and conversation takeover.
3. Click **Send Invitation**.

### Accepting the Invitation

1. The invitee receives an automated email with a secure invitation link.
2. Clicking the link prompts them to:
   - Log in with an existing Cerebronix account, or
   - Register a new account using the invited email address.
3. Upon registration and verification, the organization automatically appears in their workspace switcher dropdown.

</Steps>

## Managing Team Seats and Members

### Changing a Member's Role

To adjust a colleague's responsibilities:
1. Navigate to **Team Members** (`/team`).
2. Locate the team member in the list.
3. In their row, click the **Role** dropdown menu.
4. Select the new role (e.g., changing from `viewer` to `admin`). The permission change takes effect immediately.

### Deactivating a Member

When a colleague departs or no longer needs access:
1. In the **Team Members** list, find the user's entry.
2. Click the **Deactivate** button.
3. Confirm the deactivation. The user's active session is terminated, access to the organization is revoked, and the occupied seat is freed for reuse.

<Callout type="warn">
  **Last Owner Protection:** Cerebronix enforces a safety rule preventing the last remaining `Owner` of an organization from being deactivated or downgraded. You must promote another member to `Owner` before demoting or removing yourself.
</Callout>

## Understanding Seat Limits by Plan

Every Cerebronix organization has a seat allowance determined by its subscription tier:

- **Free Evaluation:** 1-2 administrative seats for initial testing and configuration.
- **Growth:** Expanded seat allocation for small teams managing support and sales.
- **Scale & Enterprise:** High-volume or unlimited seat allowances supporting larger multi-shift contact centers.

If you attempt to invite a member when all seats are occupied, the console displays:
> *"Seat limit reached. To add more team members, upgrade your plan on the Billing page."*

To add seats, an `Owner` can navigate to **Billing** (`/billing`) and select an upgraded plan.

## Troubleshooting Invitations

| Issue | Cause | Solution |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Invited user did not receive the email** | The invitation email was filtered by a corporate spam filter, or a typo occurred in the address. | Have the user inspect their spam/junk folder. You can re-send the invitation from the Team Members table. |
| **"Seat limit reached" error when sending invite** | The organization has exhausted the allowed seats on its current tier. | An `Owner` can upgrade your plan under **Billing** (`/billing`) or deactivate an inactive member to free up a seat. |
| **Member cannot see the organization after accepting** | The user signed in with a different email address than the one invited. | Confirm the invited email address matches the user's active Cerebronix login email exactly. |
| **Cannot demote or remove a team member** | The target member is the sole remaining `Owner`. | Assign another member the `Owner` role first, then proceed with the adjustment. |
