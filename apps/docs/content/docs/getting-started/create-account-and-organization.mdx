This guide walks you through registering an administrator account, verifying your identity, and establishing your organization workspace on Cerebronix.

**Role Assignment:** The user who registers the initial organization is automatically assigned the **Owner** role with full administrative and billing privileges.

---

## Prerequisites

Before beginning setup, ensure you have:
- A modern desktop web browser navigating to [cerebronix.com](https://cerebronix.com).
- A valid business email address capable of receiving verification emails.

---

## Step-by-Step Registration & Workspace Setup

<Steps>

### Step 1: Account Registration

1. Navigate to the Cerebronix registration portal at [`https://cerebronix.com/registration`](https://cerebronix.com/registration).
2. Complete the user profile form:
   - **First Name & Last Name:** Your official name as the primary account administrator.
   - **Email Address:** Your business email (this serves as your primary sign-in identifier).
   - **Password:** Enter a strong password (minimum 8 characters, containing uppercase, lowercase, numbers, and symbols).
   - **Confirm Password:** Re-enter your password to prevent typographical errors.
3. Review and check the **Terms of Service & Privacy Policy** agreement checkbox.
4. Click **Sign up**.

### Step 2: Email OTP Verification

1. Cerebronix sends a secure 6-digit one-time password (OTP) to your registered email address.
2. Check your email inbox (and spam/junk folder if not received within 60 seconds).
3. On the verification screen (`/verification`), enter the 6-digit code.
4. Click **Verify Code**. Once verified, your account authentication identity is confirmed.

<Callout type="info">
  **Didn't receive a code?** Verification codes remain valid for 15 minutes. If your code expires, click **Resend verification code** on the verification screen.
</Callout>

### Step 3: Register Your Business Organization

After identity verification, the onboarding wizard prompts you to define your business organization workspace:

1. Fill out the business organization profile:
   - **Organization Name:** Enter your official business name (e.g., `Apex Retail Global`).
   - **About Organization:** Provide a brief 1-2 sentence description of your business domain and customer support objectives.
   - **Company Size:** Select your team tier (e.g., `1-10`, `11-50`, `51-200`, `201+` employees).
   - **Industry:** Select your operating sector (e.g., `Retail & E-commerce`, `Technology & SaaS`, `Healthcare`, `Financial Services`).
   - **Business Type:** Choose between `B2B`, `B2C`, or `Both`.
   - **Country:** Select your primary operating country.
   - **Phone Number:** Enter your primary business contact number.
   - **Default Currency:** Choose your operating currency (e.g., `USD`, `EUR`, `GBP`, `INR`).
2. Click **Submit Registration**.

<Callout type="warn">
  **Currency Selection Impact:** Your organization's default currency directly governs product catalog prices, shipping region rates, and order invoices across the native Cerebronix Store. Ensure you select your primary settlement currency during initial onboarding.
</Callout>

</Steps>

## Working with Multiple Organizations

Cerebronix natively supports multi-tenant operators. A single user login can create or join multiple distinct organizations:

- **Switching Workspaces:** Click the organization name in the top-bar navigation header to open the workspace switcher dropdown, then select the desired workspace.
- **Creating an Additional Organization:** Open the workspace switcher dropdown and click **+ Create New Organization** (`/organization/create`). Each workspace operates with strictly isolated knowledge sources, AI agents, billing ledgers, and channel integrations.

## Troubleshooting Common Registration Issues

| Issue | Root Cause | Actionable Resolution |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **"Email already registered" error** | An account already exists under this email address. | Navigate to [`cerebronix.com/signin`](https://cerebronix.com/signin) and log in, or click **Forgot password** to initiate password recovery. |
| **Email verification code rejected** | The code has expired or was entered incorrectly. | Click **Resend Code** to trigger a fresh 6-digit OTP, ensuring you submit it within the 15-minute validity window. |
| **"Closed Beta waitlist" notice** | Your organization has registered during an approval-gated preview phase. | Platform access is granted sequentially by our provisioning team. Contact [`support@cerebronix.com`](mailto:support@cerebronix.com) for expedited enterprise evaluation access. |
