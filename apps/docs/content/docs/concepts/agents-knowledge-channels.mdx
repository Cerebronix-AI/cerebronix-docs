---
title: Agents, knowledge, and channels
description: How agents, data sources, and channels relate.
---

A **data source** is what the agent can read. A **channel** is where a
customer talks. An **agent** is the configuration that sits between them
(instructions, binds).

Create the source and the channel first when you can. Then create or bind
the agent. Public `POST /agents/{id}/ask` spends `public_ask_agent`
credits. It does not call a Chatwoot agent-bot.
