Cerebronix provides comprehensive ecommerce capabilities to power AI-driven sales dialogues, real-time product discovery, and frictionless chat checkouts.

## Native Store Management

For businesses without an external ecommerce platform, Cerebronix includes a native store management system accessible directly from the **Ecommerce** section in the operator console:

- **Store Setup:** Configure store profiles, default currency, and regional fulfillment settings.
- **Product Catalog:** Create and manage products, image galleries, descriptions, categories, and multi-variant SKUs (sizes, colors, materials).
- **Inventory & Fulfillment:** Track stock levels across storage locations and manage shipping options.
- **Orders & Payments:** Oversee customer orders, fulfillment workflows, and supported payment gateway configurations.

## Conversational Shopping Integration

Once your product catalog is active—either via the Cerebronix Native Store or an external integration such as Shopify—your AI Agents can automatically:
- Answer customer inquiries regarding product specifications, sizing, and availability.
- Provide tailored product recommendations based on customer preferences.
- Generate direct checkout links within WhatsApp, Instagram, or Web Chat conversations.
