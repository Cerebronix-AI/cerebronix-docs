When you finish this page, you have opened **Billing** and you can read
the Free empty state.

**Who:** `owner` or `admin`.

## Prerequisites

An organization. Do not enter a real payment card in sandbox or demonstration workspaces.

## Steps

1. Open **Billing** from the sidebar, or **View billing** on the
   dashboard.
2. Read **Overview**. Tabs are **Overview**, **Plan**, **Invoices**, and
   **Usage**.
3. The live ledger is the current credit balance. Wallet rows can lag up
   to five minutes. **Recent payments** is empty on a new org.

![Apex Retail Global Billing Overview showing live ledger balance, auto-recharge settings, and payment history.](https://docs-cdn.cerebronix.com/guides/billing/01-overview.png)

Expected result: you can tell ledger balance from plan limits on the
dashboard.

Free and Starter cannot mint public API keys even after a top-up.
