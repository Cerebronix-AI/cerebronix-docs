Cerebronix connects directly with leading CRM and ecommerce platforms to power personalized AI dialogues, real-time product recommendations, and instant cart checkouts.

## Supported Integrations

- **Shopify:** Flagship ecommerce integration supporting live product catalog indexing, inventory availability checks, and direct checkout URL generation.
- **WooCommerce & Magento:** Connect your existing ecommerce stores via API credentials to enable conversational shopping.
- **HubSpot:** Synchronize customer contacts, timeline events, and lead attribution with your CRM.

## Connecting a Platform

1. In the Cerebronix console, navigate to **Integrations** in the sidebar.
2. Select your provider card and click **Connect**.
3. Authorize the connection via OAuth or input your store API credentials. Once connected, catalog data synchronizes automatically.
