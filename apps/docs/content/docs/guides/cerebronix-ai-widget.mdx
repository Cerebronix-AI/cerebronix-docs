When you finish this page, you know the in-console widget from the
empty-state screenshots and you do not confuse it with a public site
embed.

**Who:** any signed-in operator.

## In the console

Every operator page shows a **Knowledge Bank AI** bubble
("Replies under a minute"). That is the signed-in test widget, not
`loader.js` on your marketing site.

The same bubble appears on Dashboard, Chat, and Channels in the
getting-started shots.

## Site embed

Embedding the visitor widget on an external origin is a channel bind
plus the chat-ui loader. Phase D covers that path. Do not copy a
production `loader.js` snippet here.

If the bubble is missing, you are not on the signed-in console, or a
client error replaced the page.
