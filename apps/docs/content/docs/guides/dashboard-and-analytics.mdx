When you finish this page, you can read the Docs Demo dashboard tiles and
you know why **Analytics** is locked on Free.

**Who:** any org role can open the dashboard. `owner` or `admin` can
upgrade.

## Prerequisites

A signed-in operator session on [cerebronix.com](https://cerebronix.com).

## Steps

1. Open **Dashboard**. The heading displays your organization name.
2. Review **Credits & billing** and **Usage & plan limits**. A Free tier organization provides 200 introductory credits, 1 data source, and 2 channels.
3. Open **Analytics** in the sidebar. On Free it shows **Analytics is a Growth plan feature** and an **Upgrade Plan** option.

![Docs Demo dashboard. Credits, usage limits, and conversation analytics locked until Growth.](https://docs-cdn.cerebronix.com/guides/getting-started/01-dashboard-empty.png)

![Analytics page on Free. Growth upgrade gate and Upgrade Plan button.](https://docs-cdn.cerebronix.com/guides/analytics/01-growth-locked.png)

If credits show `0` and **You're out of credits**, top-up or wait for an
admin grant. The public API still returns `403 public_api_disabled` on
Free even when credits are non-zero.
