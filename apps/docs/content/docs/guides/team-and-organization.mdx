When you finish this page, you have opened **Organizations** and you
know where invite is missing.

**Who:** `owner` can edit the organization. `owner` or `admin` can grant
roles when an invite path exists.

## Steps

1. Open **Organizations** (`/organization`) to review or update organization profile details (name, industry, address, and website).
2. To manage team members and invites, open **Team Members** (`/team-members`).
3. View active organization members, their assigned roles, and current seat utilization against your plan quota.
4. Select **+ Invite** to invite new teammates via email and assign their role (`admin`, `developer`, or `viewer`).

![Apex Retail Global Organizations card showing verified organization details and editing options.](https://docs-cdn.cerebronix.com/guides/team/01-organization.png)

Expected result: you can manage organization profile settings under Organizations and manage team access and invitations under Team Members.
