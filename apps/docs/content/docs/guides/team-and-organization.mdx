When you finish this page, you have opened **Organizations** and you
know where invite is missing.

**Who:** `owner` can edit the organization. `owner` or `admin` can grant
roles when an invite path exists.

## Steps

1. Open **Organizations** (`/organization`).
2. Confirm the org card: name, industry, type, size, about, address,
   website, phone, and **Edit**.
3. Do not look for Invite here. `/teams` is agent teams
   (`+ Create New Team`), not org members.
