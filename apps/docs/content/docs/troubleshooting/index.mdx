When you finish this page, you can match a common failure to one check.

## Session cookie does not stick

Local cookies use `COOKIE_DOMAIN=cerebronix-dev.cerebronix.com`. Open
`https://cerebronix-dev.cerebronix.com`, not `http://localhost:3000`.

## Closed Beta waitlist after you verify email

Email verification is not the same as admin enable. An enabled user can
still hit a cached `enabled: false` in saas-ui Redis. Ask an admin to
enable the account, then sign in again.

## Public `/v1` returns 404

Bare `GET /v1/conversations` is 404. Org-scoped routes live under
`/v1/organizations/{org}/...`. Unauthenticated `/v1/me` is 401, not 404.

Use `https://public-api.cerebronix.com` or local `:8010`. Do not call
Port A (`:8000` / `api.cerebronix.com`) as the public API.

## Free org cannot mint a key

`403 public_api_disabled` is expected on Free and Starter. Upgrade to
Growth or higher.

## Oathkeeper 401 in the console

The session cookie is missing, expired, or sent to the wrong host.
Re-open the `cerebronix-dev` origin and sign in.

## Empty Tempo

Local OTLP is a no-op when the Tempo endpoint is empty. Missing traces
are not a product bug on a laptop without Tempo.

## Data Sources says Connection lost
