Effective permission is role ∩ scopes ∩ entitlement. Missing any one
fails closed.

## Roles

Keto relations on the `organization` namespace:

| Role | Who gets it | Satisfies |
|---|---|---|
| `owner` | User who creates the organization | owner, and therefore admin, developer, viewer |
| `admin` | Granted later | admin, developer, viewer |
| `developer` | Default on invite | developer, viewer |
| `viewer` | Granted later | viewer |
| `member` | Written with owner or invite | Session Oathkeeper only. Not a public API role. |

Create organization writes `owner` + `member`. An invite writes
`developer` + `member`. Viewer tokens cannot send, handoff, resolve, or
write groups.

## Public API entitlement

Numbers come from product entitlements. Currency suffixes do not change
the values.

| Plan family | PublicAPI | PublicAPI rpm | Monthly credits | Data sources | Channels |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `free` | false | 0 | 200 (hard stop) | 1 | 2 |
| `starter` | false | 0 | 1500 (pay as you go) | 4 | 4 |
| `growth` / `growth_v2` | true | 60 | 5000 (pay as you go) | 10 | 8 |
| `scale` | true | 300 | 12000 (pay as you go) | 25 | unlimited |
| `enterprise` | true | unlimited | unlimited | unlimited | unlimited |
| lookup failure | false | 0 | 200 (hard stop) | 1 | 2 |

Free and Starter cannot mint keys or call business routes
(`403 public_api_disabled`). Growth, Scale, and Enterprise can. An
unknown plan or a failed Lago lookup uses the fail-closed default
(`PublicAPI` false).

Do not document the grandfathered `growth` (₹0) plan as a buyable SKU.
